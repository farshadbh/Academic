## Caveats — read before republishing

- **United States entries are funded PhD admission routes, not named scholarships.** In the US, PhD funding comes as research/teaching assistantships or fellowships with admission. 16 of the 18 US entries are the department's official funded PhD admissions page (Fall 2027 deadlines); only 2 are named lab positions (Georgia Tech RICAL, UT Austin COMPOUND Flood Lab). Purdue notes funding is offered to only ~25% of highly competitive PhD students recommended for admission.
- **KTH (Sweden): closing dates unknown.** The KTH Varbi pages fetched as text omit the closing-date field. All 8 KTH listings were live and freshly crawled 14–19 Nov 2026 and are treated as open, but each Varbi page should be re-checked for its real deadline before publishing.
- **UK closing dates from FindAPhD mirrors:** Imperial coastal-cliff (2026-12-18), Manchester (2027-01-15), Sheffield (2027-01-13) and Southampton AI low-carbon concrete (2027-08-31) — titles, funding and eligibility were verified on the official university pages; the dates come from the listings as mirrored on FindAPhD, corroborated across multiple crawls. Cambridge dates (13/14 Apr 2027) are from Cambridge's own official jobs list.
- **Lower-confidence listings:** Nottingham grain-interface (page last crawled ~341 days ago, 2025/26 stipend £20,780) and Southampton marine-durability (2026/27 entry-cycle page, rolling, no closing date stated). Re-check both before publishing.
- **Adelaide University:** both scholarships' apply links point to Prof. Alex (Ching-Tai) Ng's official profile page — the scholarships.adelaide.edu.au links resolve to a generic scholarship search, so the supervisor profile is the official page that lists them. No stipend amount is stated on the official page (aggregators quote figures; not used).
- **UNSW dams page** is a project news page advertising PhD roles, and the **UWA flowlines entry** is an open PhD project listing with no scholarship value stated — both are project pages rather than formal scholarship pages.
- **Toronto Digital Timber** says 'preferably January 2026' start, but the page is still live and states applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until filled, so it is kept with no closing date.
- **RMIT legacy fix:** the one pre-existing listing (Jooble link, wrong closing date 2026-12-06, no stipend) was verified against rmit.edu.au and corrected: stipend A$33,826, closes 31 Dec 2026, official apply URL. A second RMIT Structures and Materials scholarship (Damage Tolerant Hybrid Composite Materials) was also verified live on rmit.edu.au and added; its page states no stipend or closing date.
- **Excluded on purpose:** listings seen only on social media or aggregators with no official university page (e.g. McGill IVADO PhD on LinkedIn, UBC UHPFRC positions on Instagram/Facebook, Monash underground-infrastructure PhDs on Facebook, Melbourne–IIT Kanpur seismic-masonry PhD on LinkedIn/Instagram, Bath SHM/digital-twin and Birmingham offshore-wind studentships on FindAPhD only), and all expired listings (e.g. Monash ultra-low-carbon concrete closed 15 Apr 2026; UQ bridge fatigue and fire safety closed Apr 2026; TU Delft steel fatigue 11 Oct 2026; Politecnico di Torino 42nd-cycle 2nd session closed 16 Nov 2026).
- **Generic all-field scholarships were excluded** (RTP, Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships, ANU PhD Scholarship, etc.) — only structural/civil-specific listings are included.
